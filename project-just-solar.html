Featured Project 01 � Solar & Renewable Energy

Just Solar Co

"SEO-focused content and optimization work designed to improve search visibility and strengthen the website's organic presence within the solar and renewable energy space."

SEO Content Strategy Keyword Research On-Page SEO Content Optimization Organic Growth Strategy

01. Project Overview

Just Solar Co is dedicated to delivering residential and commercial clean energy solutions. The objective was to build a comprehensive SEO foundation, aligning site architecture and informational content with high-intent solar searches.

02. The SEO Challenge

The solar sector is highly competitive with intense competition from national aggregators and regional installers. The website needed targeted content hubs, optimized metadata, clear internal linking, and strategic topical clustering around solar installation, ROI calculations, and incentive queries.

03. Strategy & Intent Mapping

  • ? Comprehensive keyword intent mapping for commercial and residential solar buyers.
  • ? Creation of structured topical clusters covering solar efficiency, installation timelines, and financing options.
  • ? Technical on-page optimization including schema markup for service areas and FAQ rich snippets.

04. Execution & Implementation

  • ? Conducted complete audit of existing service pages and content gaps.
  • ? Engineered comprehensive informational guides targeting informational and commercial search intent.
  • ? Optimized header tags (H1-H3), image alt texts, meta titles, and conversion-focused calls to action.

05. Content & Optimization Approach

Content Approach: Focused on high-clarity, trustworthy educational content addressing common homeowner hesitations (roof compatibility, battery storage, ROI payback periods), establishing domain expertise without promotional fluff.

Technical Optimization: Streamlined page load speed, implemented semantic structured data (Service, FAQPage schema), and cleaned internal link architecture to direct equity toward core regional service landing pages.
