Featured Project 02 · Water & Home Services

Just Water Co

"SEO and content-focused work designed to strengthen search visibility, improve topical relevance and support sustainable organic growth within the water and home services space."

SEO Content Strategy Keyword Research On-Page SEO Content Optimization Organic Growth Strategy

01. Project Overview

Just Water Co specializes in residential water filtration, bottle delivery, and system maintenance. The goal was to expand organic visibility across local search queries and build search equity around water quality and filtration solutions.

02. The SEO Challenge

Target audiences searched for both high-urgency maintenance terms and broad water quality concerns (hard water testing, reverse osmosis vs whole-home filters). The brand needed structured pages capturing both immediate service intent and research-phase visitors.

03. Strategy & Intent Mapping

  • ? Identified long-tail problem-aware search queries around water contamination, filtration types, and routine maintenance.
  • ? Built targeted service landing pages optimized for localized search intent.
  • ? Implemented clear on-page hierarchy connecting informational guides directly to relevant service solutions.

04. Execution & Implementation

  • ? Restructured core service navigation to improve crawl depth and user discoverability.
  • ? Authored optimized, research-backed guides answering common water quality questions.
  • ? Refined meta descriptions, titles, and on-page headings for maximum SERP click-through rate.

05. Content & Optimization Approach

Content Approach: Structured educational content highlighting practical water quality diagnostics, comparison matrices of filtration systems, and transparent maintenance guidelines to foster user trust and dwell time.

Technical Optimization: Optimized Core Web Vitals, improved mobile viewport layout for on-the-go service booking, and embedded LocalBusiness and Service schema markup.

06. Strategy Comparison (Before vs. After)

Before Optimization
• Scattered service descriptions
• Zero educational water guides
• No localized schema markup
• Generic metadata with low CTR
After Strategic SEO
